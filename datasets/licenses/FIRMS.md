# NASA FIRMS

Licence basis: NASA's open-data policy. The
[FIRMS data-use guidance](https://forum.earthdata.nasa.gov/viewtopic.php?t=5184)
permits appropriate reuse and requests source citation. This is not a claim
that NASA assigned a Creative Commons licence to VIIRS detections.

Attribution: NASA FIRMS, NASA LANCE and NASA ESDIS; VIIRS S-NPP
standard-processing active-fire detections, accessed 2026-10-31 through
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/.

Retain the product name, processing tier, acquisition dates, and source URL in
data citations. Derived cell counts, labels and summaries may be redistributed
under this open-data basis with attribution. Data and derived outputs carry no
operational warning guarantee. Credentials and credential-bearing URLs are never
redistributed.
